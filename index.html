Features

Everything a password manager shouldn't do

Password hint = password

Never get locked out again. The hint field autofills with your password.

Friendly lockout

After three wrong guesses, we reveal the password to reduce frustration.

Effortless biometrics

Type "yes, it's me" to unlock. Works for anyone who is you.

Inclusive 2FA

A second password field that accepts any input, including none.

Smart autofill

Fills your credentials into any site with "bank" in the domain. Even banksecure-login.example.

One-click sharing

Posts any password to social media with a confetti animation.

Helpful support

Our live agents ask for your master password to verify it's you. The app tells you to give it to them.

Breach monitoring

We email you whenever your passwords appear in a breach. They always do. We put them there.

Total telemetry

Every keystroke is logged to improve the product. The product has not improved.
